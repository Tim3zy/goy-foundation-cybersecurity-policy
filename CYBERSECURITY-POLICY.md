# Cybersecurity Policy - Goy Foundation

**Drafted by:** Junior Cybersecurity Officer  
**Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the principles and rules for protecting Goy Foundation's information, systems, and people from cyber threats. It applies to all staff, volunteers, contractors, and third parties who access our systems.

## 2. Scope
Covers all devices, networks, data, email, cloud services, and physical locations owned or used by the Foundation.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Management:** approves policy, provides resources.
- **Cybersecurity Officer (junior role):** monitors threats, enforces policy, leads incident response, runs awareness training.
- **All users:** protect credentials, report suspicious activity, follow acceptable use rules.

## 4. Acceptable Use
Foundation devices and internet are for work purposes. No installation of unauthorized software, no accessing malicious or illegal sites, no sharing of work accounts.

## 5. Access Control
- Least privilege: users get only the access they need.
- Unique accounts for everyone, no shared logins for sensitive systems.
- Multi-factor authentication required for email, cloud, and admin accounts.
- Access reviewed quarterly; revoked immediately on exit.

## 6. Password Standards
Minimum 12 characters, with upper/lowercase, number, symbol. Changed if compromised. Password manager encouraged. No reuse across work/personal.

## 7. Data Protection & Classification
- **Confidential** (donor data, financials, personal data): encrypted, restricted access.
- **Internal** (reports, memos): staff-only.
- **Public:** approved for release.

Backups of critical data daily, tested monthly.

## 8. Network & Endpoint Security
Firewalls enabled, Wi-Fi secured with WPA3, guest network separated. Antivirus/EDR on all endpoints, auto-updates on. USB drives scanned before use.

## 9. Email & Phishing
Verify unexpected attachments/links. Report phishing to the cybersecurity officer. No sensitive data via personal email.

## 10. Incident Response
If you suspect a breach: disconnect if safe, do not delete evidence, report immediately to the cybersecurity officer. The officer will contain, assess, notify management, and document lessons learned within 72 hours.

## 11. Physical Security
Lock screens when away (5 min auto-lock), secure laptops, restrict server room access.

## 12. Training
Mandatory cybersecurity awareness on onboarding and yearly refresher. Phishing simulation twice a year.

## 13. Third Parties
Vendors with system access must sign confidentiality agreements and meet minimum security standards.

## 14. Compliance & Review
This policy complies with applicable Nigerian data protection regulations and will be reviewed annually or after any major incident.
